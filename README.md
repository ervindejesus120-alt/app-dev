# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Genre:** Sci-Fi, Horror, Drama

> 
> *"Friends don't lie."*

When a young boy vanishes in a small town, his friends and family uncover a web of government secrets, terrifying supernatural forces, and a strange girl named Eleven who holds the key to everything. Set in the 1980s, the show follows this close-knit group of kids as they navigate terrifying mysteries, face otherworldly dangers, and fight to bring one another home. I love it for its incredible nostalgia, gripping storytelling, and the deep bond between the characters that makes every moment feel both thrilling and heartfelt.
